When PCA works—and what it can miss

Eight reproducible synthetic examples. PCA is centered and uses the given feature scales. These are demonstrations, not neuroscience measurements.

All stages are shown side by side for review: data only, fitted principal components, and PCA projections. Scroll horizontally on smaller screens. Each figure is a separate file for your lecture slides.

Static panels copy a high-resolution PNG. Animated panels offer Copy GIF when supported by your browser, otherwise Download GIF. To preserve animation in Google Slides, download the GIF and use Insert → Image → Upload from computer.

3D views rotate once every 20 seconds (20 fps). One-dimensional projections place every point on a single horizontal axis; identical scores overlap. PC1 arrows start at the mean and follow the fitted component direction; their lengths are enlarged for visibility, not a variance scale. PNGs are exported at 300 DPI.
